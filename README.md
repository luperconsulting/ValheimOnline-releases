# Valheim Online — downloads

Go to [**Releases**](https://github.com/luperconsulting/ValheimOnline-releases/releases/latest) and download the Mod Manager for your computer. Open it and click **Download**, and you're done.

## Mac

Download **Valheim-Mod-Manager.zip**, unzip it and open **Valheim Mod Manager**.

- The first time, macOS will say it can't check the app. Click **Done**, then open **System Settings > Privacy & Security** and click **Open Anyway**.
- If the app shows **One step left**, click **Copy launch option**. Then in Steam, right-click Valheim > **Properties**, paste it into **Launch Options**, and close that window.

## Windows

Download **Valheim-Mod-Manager-Windows.exe** and run it. It installs BepInEx for you, and there's no Steam step.

- The first time, Windows may say it protected your PC. Click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.

## Updates

When there's an update, open the Mod Manager and click **Update**.
