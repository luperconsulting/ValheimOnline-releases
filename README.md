# Valheim Online — downloads

Mac players: go to [**Releases**](https://github.com/luperconsulting/ValheimOnline-releases/releases/latest), download **Valheim-Mod-Manager.zip**, unzip it and open **Valheim Mod Manager**. Click **Download**, and you're done.

The first time you open it, macOS will say it can't check the app. Click **Done**, then open **System Settings > Privacy & Security** and click **Open Anyway**.

When there's an update, open the Mod Manager and click **Update**.
